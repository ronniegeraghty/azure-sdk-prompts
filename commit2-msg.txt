fix(review): retry 3 times then synthesize failing checks for missing IDs

Previously, runSingleReview would drop reviewers after 2 retries if they
failed to return valid check IDs. ReviewPanelBuckets would silently drop
bucket results on error. Both caused panel size drift between runs.

Now:
- maxRetries increased from 2 to 3
- Retry prompts explicitly list missing check IDs and demand complete responses
- After 3 strikes, synthesize CriterionResult{Passed: false} for missing IDs
  instead of returning error (keeps reviewer in panel)
- Bucket failures synthesize failed checks for that bucket's IDs instead of
  dropping the bucket (keeps bucket in merged result)

This ensures panel size and MaxScore remain stable even when reviewers/buckets
fail validation, eliminating drift.
